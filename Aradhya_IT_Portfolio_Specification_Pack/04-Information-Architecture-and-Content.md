## Lesson Content Model

Every session should have:
1. Exact title
2. What this session is about
3. Important concepts
4. Key terms
5. Practical/real-world relevance
6. Learning outcomes
7. My application/reflection, where appropriate

Do not reproduce textbook paragraphs verbatim.

## Visual Content Model

Use:
- diagrams for processes;
- cards for concepts;
- timelines for journeys;
- chips for technologies;
- callouts for important terms;
- comparison layouts where useful.

Do not add stock photos just to fill space.

## Empty / Missing Data Rules

If a project screenshot, certificate or achievement is not provided:
- do not create a fake one;
- do not use a placeholder that looks like real evidence;
- omit the element.

## Footer

Keep concise:
“© 2026 Aradhya Pratap Singh · Class 10 IT Portfolio”
